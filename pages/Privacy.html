Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyChance ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our Platform. By using MyChance, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: name, email, and password (or your Google account info, if you sign up with Google).
  • Profile information: age, nationality, gender, academic level, GPA, modality preference, countries of interest, and professional interests.
  • Curriculum Vita (CV) information (Premium feature): TOEFL/IELTS status, degree completion, recommendation letters, languages, work experience, volunteer experience, leadership experience, and certifications.
  • Profile photo: the avatar image you choose to upload, including the cropped version you confirm.
  • Usage information: the opportunities you view, save as favorites, or mark as goals, and your progress on related checklists.
  • Feedback and testimonials: comments you choose to submit about your experience, which may be displayed publicly on our homepage under your name (or "MyChance User" if no name is provided).
  • Preferences: your selected language and light/dark theme.
  • Payment information (Premium plan): processed through our payment provider. We do not store your full card details on our own servers.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Calculate your match percentage with available opportunities and recommend relevant ones.
  • Show you which requirements you still need to complete for a given opportunity.
  • Let you track goals and generate a downloadable PDF of your CV.
  • Send you notifications about new opportunities, if you have opted in (Premium feature).
  • Display your name and comment publicly if you choose to submit a testimonial.
  • Improve and maintain the Platform, including troubleshooting and security.
  • Communicate with you about your account, plan, or changes to our policies.

3. Legal basis for processing

Where applicable data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on: your consent (for example, when you sign up or submit a testimonial), the necessity of processing to provide the service you requested (a contract between you and us), and our legitimate interest in operating and improving the Platform.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers that help us run the Platform, such as our database and authentication provider (Supabase) and our payment processor.
  • Institutions or organizations, only if and when you choose to apply to one of their opportunities and submit your information directly to them.
  • Legal authorities, if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • The public, only for information you explicitly choose to make public — such as a testimonial you submit voluntarily. Your CV, profile details, and goals are private and are never shared with an institution unless you apply and send them yourself.

5. Cookies and local storage

MyChance uses your browser's local storage to remember your language and theme (light/dark) preferences so the site loads correctly the next time you visit. We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Data storage and security

Your data is stored using Supabase, which provides authentication, database, and file storage services with row-level security policies restricting who can read or modify your information. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you the Platform. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain it to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information (you can edit most of this directly in your Profile).
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional features, such as testimonials or notifications.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 12.

9. Children's privacy

MyChance is intended for use by students, including those in high school who may be minors. Where you are under the age required by law to consent to data processing on your own, you should have your parent or legal guardian review this Policy and provide consent before you use the Platform. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

10. International data transfers

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we take steps to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection wherever it is processed.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the Platform or by email before the changes take effect. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to exercise any of your rights, you can reach us at @mychance_app on Instagram .
