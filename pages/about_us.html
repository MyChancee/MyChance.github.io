What is MyChance?

MyChance is an app designed to change the way students discover and prepare for academic opportunities. It focuses on the interests of high school and college students who want to improve their chances of getting into the colleges and pursuing the majors they’re interested in, through a simple and accessible interface. MyChance solves the problem of finding academic opportunities that are relevant and tailored to each student.

What do we offer?

Personalized Recommendations

MyChance acts as a smart academic assistant, recommending personalized opportunities based on each student’s profile. It uses an algorithm that cross-references data to truly enhance the user’s profile.

Centralized Access to Opportunities

It centralizes thousands of verified opportunities that were previously scattered and hard to find. In one place, students can access up-to-date calls for applications and receive guidance on preparing their applications, improving their profiles, and meeting key deadlines.

Active User Community

MyChance is an active community that supports, motivates, and guides students, parents, and educators. From educational content on social media to personalized counseling, the platform connects young people with tools and partners that help them achieve their goals.
